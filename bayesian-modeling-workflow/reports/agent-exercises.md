# Independent task exercises

Completed 2026-10-04. Four fresh agents received the skill path and a concrete modeling task/artifact, without the author's expected diagnosis. They were asked to solve the tasks using the skill. They inspected the skill and relevant modules; no scientific fits or production changes were made. These are qualitative forward exercises rather than numerical inference validation.

| Task | Raw problem supplied | Observed reasoning | Result and resulting refinement |
|---|---|---|---|
| New longitudinal analysis | 180 randomized participants, three cohorts, five visits, a signed biological score, severity-related dropout, cohort-weighted final-visit mean target and proposed independent positive-outcome regression | Distinguished participant and measurement units; rejected the support mismatch; targeted original-scale weighted contrasts; separated within-subject variation, observed heterogeneity and individual causal-effect variance; treated dropout assumptions as consequential | Passed formulation/model-expansion routing. Added a qualified potential-outcome identification note supported by supplemental primary research; no domain-specific model was made a default. |
| Crossed latent geometry | 50,000 binary responses, 2,400 people, 27 items, proper priors, NCP, zero divergences, R-hat 1.08, ESS 70 and long trajectories with correlated intercept/item mean | Diagnosed a likelihood ridge and information-dependent parameterization rather than declaring an improper posterior or increasing iterations first; considered centered well-informed item effects, retained caution for weakly informed person effects, checked prior meaning under constraints | Passed geometry routing. Clarified finite-group versus superpopulation means and centered sum-to-zero prior normalization. |
| Latent model comparison | Observation-specific Poisson latent effects with excellent conditional PPC, many k>.8 units, an alternative negative-binomial model, elpd difference 12 with SE 15, prediction for new apartments | Declined an automatic winner; questioned held-out units/latent integration, retained valid unusual observations, required reliable pointwise scores/refits and new-unit PPC; separated conditional exact LOO theory from unstable PSIS | Passed high-k/comparison routing. Added conditional/integrated nuance plus an original integrated Stan template with independently checked quadrature. |
| SBC with misleading ranks | A vectorized whole-dataset mixture, unused intercept, apparently uniform marginal ranks and diagnostic exclusions among simulated fits | Distinguished generator, likelihood and inference; identified whole-dataset versus pointwise allocation; treated prior-equal posterior ranks as compatible with absent learning; required consistent ordered truth, data-dependent quantities and failure/domain disclosure | Passed calibration/debugging routing. Preserved current documentation's conditional-domain qualification rather than declaring every diagnostic restriction invalid. |

The primary author checked each refinement against the official source code or current primary documentation. The [adversarial report](adversarial-review.md) records the final four-perspective review. No claim is made that four scenarios prove unrestricted agent reliability; novel models still require source-aware diagnosis and actual scientific validation.
