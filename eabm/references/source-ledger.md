## Source defects and boundaries

- E04's Markov-property explanation should not be read as absence of long-lag autocorrelation; ordinary MCMC draws can remain correlated. The diagnostic guide uses chain dependence/ESS rather than that phrasing.
- E04 contains an inverted centered/noncentered description in the divergence prose. Use the fitted sampler flags and the method/Stan documentation; do not infer which parameterization failed from that sentence.
- E07 has an explanatory paragraph referring to a quadratic model inside a Normal/Student-t example. Its early in-sample log predictive score is labeled ELPD; distinguish in-sample LPD from cross-validated ELPD in this skill.
- E10 has a home/away mismatch in prose/equations and a missing exponential link in its schematic Poisson rate. The executable football notebooks use the exponential link. Define the actual target and align named likelihood variables.
- E13 imports `simuk as sim` but calls `simuk.SBC`; its broad self-consistency language can be mistaken for equality of a single posterior and the prior. Use conditional exchangeability of truth/posterior draws, tested ranks, and the Modrák et al. method paper.
- E08 rendered/source revisions differ: the pinned source includes automatic model support and a corrected PLPD helper. Verify installed callbacks and output shapes. Do not promise that importance sampling gives exact or finite-sample unbiased LOO.
- Maximum entropy elicitation is constrained to a specified distribution family/support. It does not identify a universal objective prior. Power-scaling sensitivity uses a CJS-based local derivative, not the raw CJS distance.
- Book descriptions of in-sample PIT/coverage are useful exploratory patterns; uniformity guarantees require appropriate held-out/exchangeable or simulation designs and treatment of discreteness/dependence.
- E13 marks posterior-SBC implementation as forthcoming, while installed simuk 0.3.0 exposes conditional machinery. Record the package route as a documentation/implementation extension, not a demonstrated book recipe.
